An octopus runs on a very different design from ours.

> Its blood uses copper-rich hemocyanin instead of iron-rich hemoglobin, which turns it blue.

## Three hearts, three jobs

Two hearts push blood through the gills. The third sends it around the rest of the body. When the octopus swims, the main heart actually stops, which is one reason it prefers crawling.

## Built for cold water

Copper-based blood carries oxygen well in cold, low-oxygen seas. It is a smart fit for the deep ocean.
