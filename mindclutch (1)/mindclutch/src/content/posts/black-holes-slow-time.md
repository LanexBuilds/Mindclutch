Gravity doesn't only pull on objects. It also stretches **time itself**.

> The closer you are to a massive object, the slower your clock ticks compared to someone far away.

## Why it happens

Einstein's general relativity treats gravity as curved spacetime. Deep in that curve, time runs slower. Scientists call this *gravitational time dilation*, and it has been measured with atomic clocks on Earth.

## Even GPS cares

Satellites sit higher up, where gravity is weaker, so their clocks run slightly faster. GPS has to correct for this every day, or your map would drift by kilometers.
